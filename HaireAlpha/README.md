# HAIRE Alpha

*Focus on Forever.*

A native Android camera for photographers. It gives you a mirrorless-style shooting experience
with real exposure control, virtual prime and zoom lenses, and a restrained, natural rendering.
The default look is ALPHA NATURAL, with 24 more original looks across Creative, Film and the TAKE HAIRE Signature set.

> HAIRE Alpha is an original application and is not affiliated with Sony, Fujifilm, Leica, Kodak,
> Ilford, Harman or CineStill. Names marked "Inspired" describe an aesthetic direction only.

---

## Install it on your Galaxy (no developer tools needed)

1. On your phone, open this repository on GitHub, go to `HaireAlpha/release/` and tap
   **`HAIRE-Alpha-1.0.0-rc3.apk`**, then **Download** (the "View raw" / download button).
2. Open the downloaded file (from the notification or **My Files › Downloads**).
3. Android will ask to allow installs from your browser or My Files:
   tap **Settings → Allow from this source**, go back, and tap **Install**.
   If Samsung Auto Blocker is on, turn it off temporarily (Settings › Security and privacy › Auto Blocker).
4. Open **HAIRE Alpha**, allow **Camera**, and start shooting.
   Photos appear in **Samsung Gallery** in the **HAIRE Alpha** album (DCIM/HAIRE Alpha).

Future builds are signed with the same key, so installing a newer APK updates the app and keeps your settings.

## The 30-second tour

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Mode** (top left) | AUTO · P · A · S · M · M1 · M2 · M3 (Memory Recall: Everyday, Street, After Dark) |
| **Three dials** | Shutter · Aperture · ISO. Drag sideways for 1/3-stop clicks; tap for a precise wheel. In A/P/AUTO the shutter dial sets the *minimum* shutter speed for Auto ISO. |
| **EV scale** | Drag to set exposure compensation (in M it shows the meter). |
| **Lens** | Your camera bag. Primes lock the focal length, so you compose with your feet. Zooms get a zoom ring. ◦ marks a moderate crop and • a substantial one; lenses the phone can't do well are greyed out. |
| **Look** | Live thumbnails from your scene, in three categories, with up to 5 favourites and an intensity slider. |
| **Fn** | A quick menu of 10 tiles. Long-press a tile to change what it does. |
| **DISP** | Cycles Clean, Essential, Professional (histogram) and Composition (grid + level). |
| **MENU** | Everything else, including **Setup › Diagnostics** and **Shooting › Reset to factory settings**. |
| **Volume keys** | Shutter by default (configurable). |

## What is real and what is virtual

- **Shutter speed and ISO are real.** Every still is captured with auto-exposure off, using the exact
  shutter and ISO shown. These come from the program in A/P/S/AUTO, or from you in M. The capture result is
  read back and written to EXIF.
- **Aperture is virtual.** The phone's lens has a fixed physical aperture, which is always used for exposure
  and written to EXIF. With **virtual bokeh** turned on (tap the Aperture dial, or Fn › Virtual bokeh), the virtual aperture
  sets how much the background is blurred. This is experimental and added when the photo is saved; the viewfinder doesn't show it.
  It uses on-device subject detection (people, pets and objects through Google Play services; an offline people-only fallback).
  If no clear subject is found, the photo is saved without blur.
- **Lenses are framing.** The app picks the best physical camera and crops as little as possible. Cropping
  never adds optical resolution, and the EXIF focal length is always the real one.

## Build from source

Requirements: JDK 17+ (21 tested), Android SDK with platform 36, and internet access to Google Maven and Maven Central.

```bash
cd HaireAlpha
echo "sdk.dir=/path/to/android-sdk" > local.properties
./gradlew :imaging:test :app:testDebugUnitTest :app:lintDebug   # all tests + lint
./gradlew :app:assembleRelease      # → app/build/outputs/apk/release/app-release.apk
./gradlew :imaging:test -Pvalidate=true   # regenerates docs/validation (images + REPORT.md)
```

If Maven Central rate-limits Robolectric's `android-all` download, pre-fetch the jar into a folder
and pass `-ProbolectricDepsDir=/that/folder` (see `app/build.gradle.kts`).

Release and debug builds are signed with the public sideload key in `keystore/` (see its README).
For a store release, create a private key.
