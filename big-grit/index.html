01 The Big Grit philosophy

The Big Grit philosophy

Grit isn't something you're born with. It's something you build — one honest rep, one kept promise, one hard day at a time.

Big Grit brings an athlete's standards to men and women who want their training to match the standard they hold everywhere else — professionals, parents, competitors and anyone done with starting over. No hype. No shortcuts. Just the work, done with intent.

  1. 01

    Discipline

    Results are built in the ordinary weeks, not the motivated ones. We make showing up the default.

  2. 02

    Accountability

    Clear expectations, honest feedback and a coach in your corner who expects you back.

  3. 03

    Progression

    Every plan starts where you are — then raises the bar deliberately, so strength is earned and kept.

  4. 04

    Resilience

    Setbacks are part of the plan. You learn to adapt, recover and come back stronger.

    06 Playbooks

    ? Questions

    Before you start.

    Location, cost, the playbooks, and what happens after you reach out.

    Where does private training happen?

    In the Dallas–Fort Worth area. The exact location is arranged with Delone after your inquiry.

    Can I work with Delone if I'm not in DFW?

    Yes. Online coaching is remote and starts with his coaching application.

    What does coaching cost?

    Delone's online coaching application asks whether you're ready to invest $1,500 to $5,000 in coaching. Private training rates are confirmed with Delone directly.

    Does a playbook include coaching?

    No. Each playbook is a self-guided $25 PDF. It doesn't include check-ins or personal support from Delone.

    How do playbook checkout and download work?

    Payhip handles payment and delivers the PDF to your email.

    What happens after I apply?

    Your answers go straight to Delone. He follows up to talk through your goal and next steps. Nothing is booked until you both agree on it.
