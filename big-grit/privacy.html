Privacy policy

Plain-language template prepared for launch. Have it reviewed for your specific business, tools and location before relying on it.

Who we are

Big Grit Performance provides personal training in Dallas–Fort Worth, Texas and online coaching. This policy explains what information this website collects and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Information you submit in the coaching application: name, email, optional phone number, training preferences, goals, start timeline, any message you write, and your consent to be contacted.
  • Find Your Grit answers, if you take the assessment and then submit an application. Answers stay in your browser and are not sent unless you submit the form.
  • How you found us: the referring website and campaign tags in the link you used (for example, Instagram), attached to your application.
  • Analytics: if enabled, aggregated usage data such as pages viewed and buttons clicked, collected through Google Analytics and/or the Meta Pixel using cookies or similar technologies.

How we use it

  • To review your application and reply about coaching.
  • To schedule and prepare for consultations.
  • To understand which channels bring people to the site, and improve it.

We do not sell your personal information. We only contact you about coaching because you asked us to, and you can ask us to stop at any time.

Embedded videos

Delone's career highlights play through YouTube and Instagram. Their players load only when you press play, and those services may then set cookies under their own privacy policies.

Who processes it

We use trusted service providers to run this website: our website host and form provider (which stores application submissions), our scheduling tool if you book a consultation, and analytics providers if enabled. They process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy terms.

How long we keep it

We keep application details for as long as needed to respond and, if you become a client, for the duration of coaching and a reasonable period after. Ask us at any time to delete your information.

Your choices

  • Ask what information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it.
  • Opt out of follow-up messages by replying “stop” or emailing us.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use tools such as the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

Health information

Please don't include detailed medical information in the website form. If an injury or condition is relevant, mention it briefly and we'll discuss it privately during your consultation.

Contact

For any privacy question or request, contact Big Grit Performance through the application form on our website.
